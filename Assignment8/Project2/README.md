# Assignment 9 Reflection

## What's one HTML, CSS, or publishing skill you feel stronger in now?

### I feel more confident using Flexbox and Grid to organize different sections of a website. At first, I struggled with getting images and text to line up correctly, but I've gotten better at understanding how layouts work and how to adjust them.

## How did it feel to navigate your own site? Is there anything that could make it more intuitive or enjoyable?

### I think my website is pretty easy to navigate because the pages are organized and the navigation stays consistent. One thing that could make it more enjoyable would be adding more interactive features to help visitors explore books based on their interests.

## Where did you struggle during the project? How did you address it (or plan to next time)?

### One thing I struggled with was making sure my images and text looked good together, especially when adjusting the layout for different screen sizes. I addressed this by experimenting with Flexbox, image sizing, and media queries until everything looked more consistent.

## If you had just one more week to work on this project, what would you add, change, or refine?

### If I had another week, I would probably add more book recommendations and maybe a way for visitors to filter books based on their interests. I think this would make the Discover page more useful and engaging.

## What are you most proud of? Did anything surprise you?

### I'm most proud of how the overall design came together. I wanted PageTurn to feel welcoming and comfortable for people who don't read very often, and I think the colors, images, and layout helped achieve that. I was also surprised by how much small CSS changes could improve the appearance of the website.
