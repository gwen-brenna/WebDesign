more info on the list of flowers selected ꩜

lotus:

the lotus is known for its ability to grow through muddy waters, emerging into something more beautiful.

they symbolize spiritual growth, transformation, & purity.

the most common colors of a lotus are pink and white, but they can also be red or blue.

they're an aquatic flower.

they're known to be used in traditional medicines to promote skin health.

iris:

the iris symbolizes faith, hope, & wisdom.

they represent 25 years of marriage.

they can be found in a variety of colors, including purple, blue, yellow, and white (250 varieties).

tulip:

tulips represent love, romance, & renewal.

there are over 3,000 types of tulips.

they can be any color but blue (they do take purple shades though).

the tulip petals are edible and can be used in baking.
