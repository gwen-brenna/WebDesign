What is PageTurn?

PageTurn is a reading discovery organization created to help young adults explore books and develop a love for reading.

We know that not everyone considers themselves a reader. Sometimes it just takes finding the right book to change that.

Getting started with reading.

Start with something you already enjoy. Books can introduce you to new interests, offer an escape, or simply entertain you.

Take your time, explore different genres, and remember that reading should be enjoyable.
