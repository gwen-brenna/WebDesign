What is PageTurn?

PageTurn is a reading discovery organization created to help young adults explore books and develop a love for reading.

We know that not everyone considers themselves a reader. Sometimes it just takes finding the right book to change that.

A LITTLE HELP ALONG THE WAY

New to reading? Start here.

Follow your interests

Start with something you already enjoy. Whether it's music, romance, history, or mystery, there's probably a book for it.

Start small

You don't have to finish a book in a week. Try reading a few pages at a time and build a routine that works for you.

Don't force it

Not every book will be your favorite. It's okay to put one down and try something different.
